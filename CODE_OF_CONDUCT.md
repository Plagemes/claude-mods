# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

We expect all participants to:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing opinions and perspectives
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive language or personal attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Any other conduct that violates our inclusive values

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct will be addressed. Enforcement actions may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation of this code of conduct:

1. **Open a private security advisory** on GitHub (if sensitive)
2. **Open a GitHub issue** to report the violation
3. Contact the maintainers directly through either method

Reports will be handled confidentially and with care.
